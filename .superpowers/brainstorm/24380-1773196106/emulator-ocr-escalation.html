AI Validation Checks
PAYER_MISMATCH
Payee name on receipt ≠ customer who uploaded
e.g. "SM Retail" uploaded via Uniqlo's portal
AMOUNT_MISMATCH
Amount on receipt ≠ invoice balance
e.g. Receipt shows ₱250K but invoice is ₱285K
DATE_MISMATCH
Payment date on receipt is before invoice issue
or suspiciously far from upload date
DUPLICATE
Reference number already used in a prior payment
Same receipt uploaded twice
Gemini Vision Extraction (Prompt Sketch)

  - reference_number
  - bank_name
  - payee_name (who received the payment)
  - payer_name (who made the payment)
  - document_type (check / bank_transfer / deposit_slip)
Return as JSON."
